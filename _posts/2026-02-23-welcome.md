Welcome to the OpenCanvas blog.

We built this space to share what we're learning as we build OpenCanvas — an AI-powered desktop automation tool that lets you describe tasks in natural language and turn them into repeatable workflows.

## What is OpenCanvas?

OpenCanvas uses AI capabilities like function calling and computer vision to interact with your desktop applications. Instead of writing scripts or recording macros, you describe what you want done step by step, and AI executes it for you.

Think of it as having a capable assistant that can see your screen, understand your intent, and take action across any application.

## Why This Blog Exists

Building at the intersection of AI and desktop automation means we're constantly discovering new patterns, solving unusual problems, and rethinking how humans and AI should collaborate.

We'll share:

- **Product updates** — what we're building and why
- **Technical deep dives** — how AI function calling, vision, and workflow orchestration actually work under the hood
- **Industry insights** — where AI automation is headed, and what it means for how we work
- **Practical guides** — tutorials and patterns you can apply to your own automation challenges

Whether you're a developer, a product builder, or just curious about AI automation, we hope you'll find something useful here.
